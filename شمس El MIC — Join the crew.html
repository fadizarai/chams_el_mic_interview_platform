A side project of Microsoft Club ISSATSo

Inspired by the nostalgic Tunisian TV show Chams Alik, Chams El MIC is where our trendy, funny, crazy and creative ideas come alive. The main MIC page is polished and slow. This one is fast, weekly, and imperfect on purpose.

What it isA parody and a mirror of the shared student experience.
What it isn'tA news page. Nothing here is real news.

You can't edit videos? Send a meme. You hate being on camera? Write the script. There's a seat for everyone.

What we make

Weekly bulletin

Our fake news show: the ISSATSO weather report ("70% chance of a surprise exam"), absurd headlines, and the quote of the week overheard in the hallway.

Caught at ISSATSO

You send memes, funny moments, reels and photos. We curate and caption. This is what makes it a community, not a page run by five people.

Micro-trottoir

Chams Alik style street interviews with silly 30-second questions, filmed in the hallways, the buvette and the foyer.

House rules

  • Humor about exams, the buvette, the resto and the foyer
  • Gentle jokes about teachers only
  • Every post is reviewed by two editors
  • Credit if you want it, anonymous if you don't
  • Targeting a person without their agreement
  • Anything can be removed, just ask
